You exert less effort when you are not alone.

A 1913 study found that individuals pulling on a rope in a group exert less force than when pulling individually.

This is social loafing. As group size increases, the individual sense of accountability diminishes, leading to lower contribution per person.

Your output is often calibrated to the anonymity provided by the group.
