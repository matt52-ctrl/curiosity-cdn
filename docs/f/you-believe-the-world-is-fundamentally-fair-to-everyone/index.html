You believe the world is fundamentally fair to everyone.

A 1966 study demonstrated that people will blame victims for their own misfortune to maintain the belief that people generally get what they deserve.

Melvin Lerner and Carolyn Simmons, 1966, Journal of Personality and Social Psychology
