You adopt the mood of the room without choosing.

A 1994 research review found that people automatically mimic the facial expressions and postures of those around them.

Emotional contagion is an involuntary process. By matching the physical state of others, your nervous system begins to experience the same emotional state.

Your mood is frequently a reflection of the people you spend time with.
