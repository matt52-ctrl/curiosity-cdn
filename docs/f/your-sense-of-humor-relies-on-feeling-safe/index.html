humor

Your sense of humor relies on feeling safe.

Humor arises when a situation is perceived as a violation of social norms but is simultaneously deemed benign.

The brain requires a conflict between what should be and what is, combined with a resolution that poses no actual threat. If a violation is too severe, you feel threatened; if it is too harmless, you feel bored. You find something funny when your brain recognizes the danger is artificial.

Laughter is how you signal that a problem is not a threat.

Where this comes from

Study
McGraw and Warren (2010)
What the check found
The claim accurately describes the Benign Violation Theory, a widely recognized psychological framework for humor proposed by McGraw and Warren (2010).
