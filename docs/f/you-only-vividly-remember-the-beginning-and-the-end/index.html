serial position effect

You only vividly remember the beginning and the end.

A 1962 experiment confirmed that memory recall is highest for items at the start and end of a list, with a significantly lower probability of recall for middle items.

The original text stated the middle items were 'routinely forgotten,' which implies zero retention, whereas the study demonstrates a significant statistical decrease in recall rates, not total omission.

You have a clear sense of how things start and finish, but the middle is a blur.

Where this comes from

Study
Bennet Murdock, 1962, Journal of Experimental Psychology
What the check found
While Murdock's 1962 paper is the seminal study for the serial position curve, the claim misrepresents the data by suggesting a total failure of recall for middle items rather than a relative reduction.
Confidence
Solid, with limits
