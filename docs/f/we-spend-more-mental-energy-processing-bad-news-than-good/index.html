We spend more mental energy processing bad news than good.

A 2001 review concluded that humans consistently pay more attention to, and retain, negative information over positive information.

Evolutionarily, identifying a threat was more critical to survival than identifying a benefit. This mechanism ensures we prioritize managing danger, even if the 'danger' is now just an awkward social encounter.

Our brains are better designed for survival than for satisfaction.

Where this comes from

Baumeister et al., 2001, Review of General Psychology
What the check found
