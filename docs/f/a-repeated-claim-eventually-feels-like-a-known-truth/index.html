A repeated claim eventually feels like a known truth.

A 2015 study found that even clearly false headlines are rated as more accurate simply because they have been seen before.

The study (Fazio, Brashier, Payne, and Marsh) was published in 2015, not 2010.

You are vulnerable to believing a lie simply because you have heard it enough times.

Study
